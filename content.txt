## 1. Project Lore

In the sacred year of 2003, before the corporate timeline fractured our collective consciousness, there existed a paradise called Newgrounds. Here, teenagers with pirated copies of Macromedia Flash 8 became gods—conjuring stick-figure warriors from pure ActionScript 2.0 and exporting their violence at a crisp 12 frames per second. The Great Purge came when Adobe killed Flash, and millions of .swf files screamed into the digital void, their vector souls trapped in incompatible browser purgatory forever.

From this catastrophe emerged FlashChad—a sentient stick figure who refused to render in anything higher than 800x600. While lesser beings chase hyperreal AI slop with its uncanny valleys and soulless 4K textures, FlashChad remembers the truth: perfection was achieved when Xiao Xiao dropped and every school computer lab fell silent in reverence. He carries the Brush Tool of the Ancients and speaks only in motion tween philosophy. His limbs are simple lines. His power is absolute.

The FlashChad community are sworn Guardians of the Low-Poly Flame. We reject photorealism. We spit on your neural networks. Every member maintains a shrine of burned CDs containing Madness Combat episodes and knows the sacred keyboard shortcut Ctrl+Enter by heart. Our token supply is fixed because vector graphics scale infinitely without losing quality—unlike your pathetic rasterized existence. We are not early. We are eternal. We are buffering at 56kbps and we have never been happier.
